feat(vraelis): reframe product as an AI revenue agent

Shifts the product from "a chatbot/widget you configure" to "an AI sales
agent you create for your business," working leads across every channel.

- Agent identity: name + voice/tone (friendly/professional/direct/casual)
  feed the live AI system prompt across web chat, email, and SMS, so the
  agent introduces itself by name and speaks in the chosen tone. Stored in
  new fail-soft agent_name + agent_tone columns.
- "Your agent" summary card atop the Inbox: name, role line (qualifies,
  follows up, books, collects payment), and channel chips that light up as
  capabilities turn on.
- Channels reframed as capabilities, not integrations: "Text and voice"
  (not "set up Twilio"), Email, Website and chat, Book calls, Take payments.
  Twilio stays the backend untouched; the word no longer appears in the UI.
- Lead conversation thread now labels agent messages with the agent's name
  instead of a generic "Vraelis".
- Onboarding reads "Create your revenue agent, what it sells, channels,
  payments" instead of filling out settings.
- Add-lead and empty-state copy attribute the work to "your agent".

Requires vraelis_workspaces.agent_name + agent_tone columns (migrated and
verified in prod before this deploy). Reviewed by a 10-agent fan-out.

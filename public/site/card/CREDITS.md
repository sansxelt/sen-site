# Card pictures: sources

Twenty-seven card pictures: twenty-six made on 2026-10-02 by the images batch (Batch 0), and `pricing-16x10.jpg`,
added by the final review on 2026-10-03 (UTC) for the Pricing card on /solutions/agencies (its row, last, says how it
was made): 4:3 at 1600x1200 (the solutions index) and 16:10 at 1600x1000 (CrossLinks). The eight Solutions entries have both sizes (defense, fleets, commerce,
public-sector, ai-built-apps, saas, agencies, enterprise); the four use cases have both sizes (A7.5 names only their
16:10; the 4:3 were made because the Batch 0a brief asked for them, and nothing uses them yet); /agents and
/developers have a 16:10 only, because they are CrossLinks targets but not on the solutions index. /integrations is
never a CrossLinks target, so it has no card. Every row below gives the file, what it shows, its source, its author
and licence, what was checked, what was masked and its mean luminance (plan A7.4). Luminance is the Rec. 709 luma of
the encoded values, as `SP/qa/imagecheck.mjs` measures it; the black point is the 0.5th percentile of that luma. SP
is the session scratchpad.

**The render (fleets).** Crops of the same still as `hero/fleets.jpg`, with the same grade (see `hero/CREDITS.md`).

**Compositions (all the others).** Each card is one panel on the FrameHero panel-kind ground (#0E0E10 with one soft
light) in a MediaPanel-style shell with a words-only bar, running off the bottom edge; no window dots and no
decorative dots. Rendered at 480x360 and 480x300 CSS px, device pixel ratio 10/3, in IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono
(`app/fonts`), saved as JPEG quality 82, 4:4:4. What is inside the shell is one of three things:
- a crop of a fixture or demo-app capture, or of a recorded screenshot, pixels unchanged, only scaled to the shell
  (the recorded screenshots are 1x, so in these 1600 px files they are scaled up to 2.1 times; a card is shown at a
  quarter to a half of the file's width, depending on the screen). Captures carry "Captured 2026-10-02, not from
  the run" in their bar; recorded evidence carries its app, run and date. Scripts: `SP/qa/b0/comp0a.html`,
  `SP/qa/b0/comp0a.mjs`, `SP/qa/b0/build_comps.py`;
- a crop of a masked console capture from `public/site/hero` (enterprise, agencies; see `hero/CREDITS.md`);
- a coded panel: a static mock of the panel the page draws, with real data read out of the source files, never
  retyped (public-sector, agents, developers). Scripts: `SP/qa/b0/panels.html`, `SP/qa/b0/render-panels.mjs`,
  `SP/qa/b0/finalize.py`.

IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`, https://openfontlicense.org).

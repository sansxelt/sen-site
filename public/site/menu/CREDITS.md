# Menu pictures: sources

Twelve 1400x700 pictures (2:1, shown at most 705x352 in the mega menus), all made on 2026-10-02 by the images batch
(Batch 0) from the same sources as the page heroes, so each menu picture previews its page: the eight Solutions
entries (defense, fleets, commerce, public-sector, ai-built-apps, saas, agencies, enterprise), how-a-check-works,
and the Product menu's agents, developers and integrations. Every row below gives the file, what it shows, its
source, its author and licence, what was checked, what was masked and its mean luminance (plan A7.4). Luminance is
the Rec. 709 luma of the encoded values, as `SP/qa/imagecheck.mjs` measures it; the black point is the 0.5th
percentile of that luma. SP is the session scratchpad.

**The render (fleets).** A crop of the same still as the fleets scene hero, with the same grade (see
`hero/CREDITS.md`).

**Compositions (all the others).** Each picture is one panel on the FrameHero panel-kind ground (#0E0E10 with one
soft light, plan A5) in a MediaPanel-style shell with a words-only bar, running off the bottom edge; no window dots
and no decorative dots. Rendered at 700x350 CSS px, device pixel ratio 2, in IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono
(`app/fonts`), saved as JPEG quality 82, 4:4:4. What is inside the shell is one of three things:
- a crop of a fixture or demo-app capture, or of a recorded screenshot, pixels unchanged, only scaled to the shell
  (defense, how-a-check-works, commerce, saas, ai-built-apps). Captures carry "Captured 2026-10-02, not from the
  run" in their bar; recorded evidence carries its app, run and date. Scripts: `SP/qa/b0/comp0a.html`,
  `SP/qa/b0/comp0a.mjs`, `SP/qa/b0/build_comps.py`;
- a crop of a masked console capture from `public/site/hero` (enterprise, agencies; see `hero/CREDITS.md`);
- a coded panel: a static mock of the panel the page draws, with real data read out of the source files by
  `SP/qa/b0/extract-data.mjs`, never retyped (agents, developers, integrations, public-sector). Scripts:
  `SP/qa/b0/panels.html`, `SP/qa/b0/render-panels.mjs`, `SP/qa/b0/finalize.py`.

# Vraelis independent product review

For consequential product, market, security architecture or investment decisions,
the user requests independent adversarial review. Use separate agents for the
strongest reasonable case for and against, then independent reviewers with
distinct perspectives who see both arguments but not each other's conclusions.
Use twelve perspectives for major company-direction decisions; keep smaller
decisions proportional. Routine UI fixes do not need this process.

Follow [the product review method](docs/strategy/product-review-method.md).
Check actual implementation and existing alternatives, preserve substantive
dissent, and resolve the decision through evidence and falsifiable experiments.
AI agreement is not customer validation. Review conclusions do not authorize
outreach, spending, publication or opening private product access; follow the
authorization already present in the user's task for implementation.

# Company and product names

Use Vraelis as the company and display name. The user dropped the suffix discussion;
do not propose or adopt another suffix unless they reopen it.
Vraelis Contour is the first product direction, initially for release/platform
and security engineers at robotics suppliers and integrators. Its current
engineering foundation is a private reference experiment, not a production
platform. Different product names follow distinct customer jobs and requirements,
never private versus public access. Use the full Vraelis Contour name in product
references. Follow [the naming decision](docs/strategy/company-and-product-naming.md)
and do not infer a registered legal-name change or shipping capabilities.

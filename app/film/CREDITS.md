# Homepage film: sources

The film at `public/home/film.mp4` (with the vertical phone cut `film-vertical.mp4` and the two posters,
`film-poster.jpg` and `film-poster-vertical.jpg`) is composed from the pieces below. No credit is required by any of these licences; it is kept so the source of every frame is
known. The mega menus' pictures have their own list, `public/home/menu/pics/CREDITS.md`.

## Rendered by Vraelis
- The take: our own drone model (`app/dev-preview/v6/_system/drone-model.tsx`, satin finish), rendered by
  `app/film/orbit` as one continuous shot: it takes off from a pad in a garage and circles the camera while the
  place changes, holding one place and one size in the frame throughout (v4, 2026-10-01). The four places are 360 degree panoramas from Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain): Autoshop 01,
  Modern Buildings Night, Bambanani Sunset, Kiara 3 Morning (https://polyhaven.com), used at 16k so the
  background stays sharp at full screen. (The studio shot rendered by `app/film/drone` left the film on
  2026-10-01, with the rest of the first version the founder found simulated.)

No identifiable faces, no readable brand badges (checked at 4K before use).

How the catch is used (v4): each frame is scaled and moved so its drone sits where the rendered take leaves
ours, at the same size, and the sky above the top of the source frame is continued from the frame's own sky (a
gradient fitted to its top band, with the footage's grain). Nothing else in the frame is changed. The scripts
are in the scratchpad's `reel/v4` (track.py, catch.py, compose4.py).

The board and controller shots left the film in v4 (2026-10-01): neither had the drone in it, and the founder
asked that the drone hold its place through every change. Two of the mega menus' pictures are still frames of
them (credited in `public/home/menu/pics/CREDITS.md`).

The laptop, pickup and airliner shots were cut on 2026-10-01: the laptop read as staged, and the vehicle shots
showed nothing being checked.

The four menu loops (Pexels) that were credited here left the site on 2026-10-01, when the menus took one
picture per link instead, as scale.com's do.

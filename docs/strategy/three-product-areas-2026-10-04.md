# Product areas: Defense, Infrastructure, Robotics

Founder direction, October 4, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Builds on the physical-system competitor and recording-adapter research already in this directory.

These are three application areas for a shared verification foundation, not three independently proven products. Public positioning leads with Defense, Infrastructure and Robotics. SDKs and developer operations support integration and release workflows. Digital application verification remains accessible as a supporting cloud workflow; it is not a fourth primary market.

| Area | Initial candidate requirement | Evidence we can evaluate today | Deployment gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Defense | The confirmed task applies only to the intended asset | Local supplied task-state recordings; approved browser runs on reachable unclassified simulations | Classified or disconnected environments, live military systems, accreditation and private-network runners are not supported |
| Infrastructure | An accepted command must not be mistaken for completed equipment work | Local task reports with declared coverage; control-panel workflow evidence | Site-specific equipment adapters, operational-network access and independent physical observations are not built |
| Robotics | A requested job reaches the reported outcome without changing another robot | Same-task and same-asset recorded event evaluation, comparison and source-hash exports | Live robot interfaces, ROS CDR/compressed MCAP, clock attestation and combined browser/API/device execution are not built |

Defense and infrastructure include public agencies, research institutions and other noncommercial or restricted operations. Eligibility, authority to access a system, data classification and deployment constraints must be resolved for each prospective pilot. No claim of universal commercial availability, authorization or safety certification follows from the site labels.

## Build order

1. Validate recorded handoff requirements with robotics or infrastructure teams who can supply authorized, non-sensitive recordings and seeded failures. Compare Vraelis against their existing scripts and log tools; a competitor's ontology or data model alone does not establish an unmet need.
2. Require useful source identity, capture coverage and consistent clocks before broadening state rules. A hash is evidence of an exported file's bytes, not attestation that its events happened.
3. Choose the next protocol decoder or live adapter from an actual pilot dataset. SDK publication follows a stable, exercised input contract; do not sell an unpublished SDK as installed connectivity.
4. Develop private or disconnected deployment only for a validated buyer requirement with an explicit authorization and data-handling model. Restricted defense requirements are not satisfied by renaming a public SaaS tool.

Success gate: the workflow detects known contradictions and missing evidence, makes fewer unsupported conclusions than the existing baseline, and saves meaningful review time on a customer's real authorized dataset. Until then, differentiation and willingness to pay remain hypotheses.

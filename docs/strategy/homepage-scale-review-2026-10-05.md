# Homepage review and implementation

Reviewed October 5, 2026. This is a presentation review, not a market-size study or evidence that the product category is unoccupied.

## References

- [Scale](https://scale.com/): reviewed the full homepage, including the opening, scroll-driven visual explanation, industry imagery, customer stories, capabilities, news and footer.
- [Foxglove defense and aerospace](https://foxglove.dev/solutions/defense-and-aerospace): connects a named audience to concrete recorded-data and live-connection workflows, then shows product and customer evidence.
- [Foretellix](https://www.foretellix.com/): defines its autonomy domain, development workflows and customer proof clearly. It demonstrates that autonomy testing is an established competitive space.

## What the comparison exposed

Vraelis repeated its promise through a paragraph card, image collage, agent section and closing scene. The result had imagery but weak progression. Scale assigns each section a different job: establish the promise, explain the mechanism visually, show applications, substantiate the claims, then provide a next step. Its credibility depends on real customer deployments and results as much as visual execution.

The useful pattern to adopt is that progression. Scale's photographs, wording, customer logos, metrics and illustrated systems are not Vraelis assets or evidence.

## Implemented

1. Preserve the opening field film and centered tagline. Finish its native-scroll sequence with a short independent-review statement and a conceptual perspective illustration connecting the requested task, reported state and device.
2. Introduce Defense, Infrastructure and Robotics through one large photographic section with keyboard-operable switches. Use the shared sector registry so these routes stay consistent with navigation.
3. Show the existing working recording evaluator next: conflicting reports, insufficient evidence and an improved result. Make the connection between control-panel, service and device records explicit.
4. Keep the dated browser simulation recording farther down. Its original evidence and simulation labels remain intact.
5. Replace repeated collage, agent and changelog sections with two useful destinations: recording documentation and the platform's actual capabilities. End with a direct route to the no-account recording workflow.
6. Credit the new Hoover Dam image and all reused photography. These images illustrate subject areas; they do not represent deployments or endorsements.

## Product limits and next evidence

The recording beta processes supported JSON and a constrained uncompressed JSON MCAP subset locally. It compares supplied reports against reviewed criteria; it does not establish physical truth, provide live device connections or certify a system. The browser workflow is a separate demonstration. The public page must preserve these distinctions.

The next credibility milestone is a consented external engineering example showing a real task across independently captured control, service and device reports, with missing evidence handled explicitly and the same criteria repeated after a fix. More dramatic imagery cannot replace that evidence. No claim of unique technology, guaranteed safety or mathematical verification follows from this review.

# Design 05 — Reference analysis

Six category-defining sites, studied live for *why* they feel exceptional (not to copy their surface). The
through-line is unanimous and becomes Vraelis's north star: **the real product artifact is the artwork; the
page leads with one immediately-understandable behavior; motion only ever explains a real state change; color is
quarantined to the one moment that matters; restraint reads as confidence.**

---

## Stripe — stripe.com
- **Signature behavior:** the hero *is* a real, working checkout (card field, Apple Pay/Klarna tabs, localized
  currency), re-localized across countries. The embeddable product is the illustration; adaptivity is the argument.
- **Functional lesson:** lead with the real artifact doing its one job at production fidelity, mid-interaction.
- **Visual lesson:** a calm high-contrast neutral field; spend saturated color on exactly one thing (the product
  UI + one signature gradient). A trust product should lean *even more* restrained — verdict color as the only load-bearing hue.
- **Hierarchy lesson:** outcome first, scale/trust second, mechanism third.
- **Must not copy:** the multi-hue gradient ribbon, Sohne, the "___ infrastructure to grow your revenue" cadence.

## Linear — linear.app
- **Signature behavior:** a real issue moving Todo → In Progress, with humans *and* an agent in one activity feed.
- **Functional lesson:** show the product performing one true unit of work; let the interface carry the argument.
- **Visual lesson:** near-monochrome, a single desaturated accent, dense product shots framed by airy margins — the
  contrast is what makes the UI read as precious.
- **Hierarchy lesson:** ruthless one-idea-per-viewport; a numbered lifecycle turns a scroll into a narrated pipeline.
- **Must not copy:** near-black + single-indigo, the "1.0 / 2.0" device, the "The [x] system for [y]" cadence.

## Cursor — cursor.com
- **Signature behavior:** the live app mid-work — task cards in "In Progress"/"Ready for Review," a run's telemetry
  ("Explored 12 files… 14-minute work duration"). The *receipt of a completed run* is the proof.
- **Functional lesson:** show the artifact of the product doing the work (run, summary, diff), so the visitor
  evaluates evidence, not adjectives. For a trust product this is the whole game.
- **Visual lesson:** restraint as confidence; strict mono-for-code / sans-for-prose split signals "real artifact."
- **Must not copy:** the "X is your Y for building ambitious software" line, teal-on-white, painterly wave backdrops.

## Notion — notion.com
- **Signature behavior:** ask in plain language, get an answer grounded in your own work, then an agent acts.
- **Functional lesson:** demonstrate the core loop as a working micro-interaction (input → system works → grounded
  output); copy only labels what the UI already proved.
- **Visual lesson:** the site and the app share one typeface and spacing rhythm, so marketing reads as *screenshots
  of the app*, not illustrations about it. This is the core of "authored."
- **Must not copy:** the "Where teams and agents Think together" cadence, the pastel block palette, the AI-blue.

## Wispr Flow — wisprflow.ai
- **Signature behavior:** "Don't type, just speak" — raw filler speech visibly resolves into clean prose. One
  observable transformation, triggerable on the page.
- **Visual lesson:** warm off-white paper (not clinical white, not default dark) + one confident accent reads as
  human and premium. ~90% paper-and-ink; color only where it means something. **(Vraelis already lives here.)**
- **Motion lesson:** reserve your most deliberate motion for the exact instant value is delivered — animate the
  before→after state change so the eye learns what the product does.
- **Must not copy:** "Don't type, just speak," the cream+single-blue as-is, the waveform/pill motif.

## Mercor — mercor.com
- **Signature behavior:** the hero *runs the marketplace* — a live roles feed with real titles, rate bands, and
  "hired recently" counters. Specificity reads as evidence; a genericized example reads as marketing.
- **Visual lesson:** hairline (1px, low-opacity) structure instead of shadows/fills; numbers treated as first-class
  typographic content (tabular figures). A document-like surface reads as credible; noise reads as compensation.
- **Hierarchy lesson:** escalate *kinds* of evidence — abstract claim → working product → named humans → research.
- **Must not copy:** "Shape the frontier of AI," the roles-feed card anatomy, the APEX framing, the serif+grotesque pairing.

---

## Synthesis → what Vraelis borrows in spirit (never verbatim)
1. **The artifact is the hero.** Vraelis's artifact is a *verification*: a claim compiling into proof obligations,
   executing against a pinned deployment, and the evidence resolving a conclusion. That console is the artwork.
2. **One observable transformation.** Ours is: *the agent says it works → Vraelis checks the live product → the
   evidence answers.* The Failed→Failed→Verified sequence is the "before→after" we animate.
3. **Color quarantined to the verdict.** Warm paper + ink everywhere; the only load-bearing color is the conclusion
   (emerald Verified, red-brown Failed). This is stronger for a *trust* product than any gradient.
4. **Hairline instrument structure + tabular numerals** (Mercor/Linear) — credible, document-like, not glassy SaaS.
5. **Site and app share one language** (Notion) — same conclusion vocabulary, same evidence objects, same mark, so
   the marketing page reads as the real product, not a poster about it.
6. **Motion explains state, never decorates.** The ring sweep and the node reveal reflect real (fixture-backed)
   step results; nothing floats or parallaxes for spectacle.
7. **Honesty as a design value.** Scoped "Verified on the checked workflow," "separate preserved historical
   records," and an explicit fixture disclaimer — the page practices the trust it sells.

# Public website reference review, 4 October 2026

Reviewed x.ai’s homepage and 29 linked public routes: Grok, Build, Bot, Imagine, Voice, Solutions, Business, Government, customer support, legal, security, API, Pricing, Company, Colossus, Careers, News, contact forms, safety, privacy, legal index, marketplace, guides, use cases and changelogs. Saved route snapshots and full-page screenshots in `/workspace/scratch/xai-reference-review`. Reviewed the documentation overview separately, including its phone navigation drawer and searchable command menu.

Observed navigation menus, pricing audience tabs, code language switching, copy feedback, light/dark presentation and phone layouts. No accounts, contact forms or private products were submitted or tested. Full-page screenshots alone do not establish every sticky transition or every nested article’s behavior.

## Applicable design decisions

- Keep one coherent visual language in the opening film. Show real systems with one centered headline; product detail belongs on the Platform page.
- Connect the real-world imagery to the actual service in the following statement: live apps and control panels, without claiming Vraelis manufactures or operates the depicted hardware.
- Use concise, specific copy and a clear destination for each action. Avoid explanatory columns beneath a visual that repeat its interface.
- Developer, business and government audiences need distinct paths and useful information, rather than a larger-volume plan alone.
- Illustrated workflows should support nearby actions. Navigation, code tabs and copy buttons must work, not merely decorate the page.
- Keep detailed controls and evidence in the product experience. Default recordings avoid loading an interactive 3D renderer on a visitor’s device.

Reference sites inform hierarchy, pacing and interaction patterns. Vraelis uses its own text, original spatial scene and independently licensed/public-domain footage. No competitor assets, code, typography or proprietary claims are reused. Product differentiation research remains separate work; this review does not establish market superiority.
